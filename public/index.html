Positions use the myRapid kiosk with government GTFS feeds as additional sources. Positions refresh every 20 seconds while this page is visible. GPS data can be delayed or unavailable. No predicted arrival times are provided. A vehicle position does not guarantee that a bus is in passenger service. Saved routes stay on this device. Your location is only requested when you tap the location button and is not sent to our server. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap.
